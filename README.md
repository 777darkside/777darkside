## About

I lead product design at Exeboard, a corporate governance SaaS for boards across Latin America, expanding into Mexico and El Salvador. I started as a fullstack developer at Edoo, an EdTech platform for schools, then moved into design and ended up leading it: the LMS redesign, the financial module (statements, bank sync, card payments).

Because I still code, I work across the whole feature cycle: research, prototyping, checking feasibility with engineering, and looking at usage data after something ships.

Based in Antigua Guatemala. Product Designer, Product Lead, sometimes still just a dev.

## Currently building

**Conversational ERP for microbusinesses** · open source, solo + AI, 2026 in progress

A voice first ERP for four family businesses in Sacatepéquez, Guatemala: a pizzeria, a burger spot, a specialty café and a hardware store. You speak or type in plain language, the model pulls out the entities and renders a pre filled form, list or table for you to check before it runs, invoicing included. Right now I'm in discovery with the pilot businesses and prototyping the UX directly in code.
